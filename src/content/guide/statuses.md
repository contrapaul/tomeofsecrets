# Statuses

A status is a marker on you or an enemy that changes what happens next. Most wear
off; some stay for the whole fight. The numbers below come straight from the game,
so they are always right.

{{statuses}}

## Three worth understanding first

**[[Weak]] on an enemy** is often better than more damage. An enemy that hits for
12 hits for 9 while Weak. Applying Weak before a big attack saves you more health
than a card that deals 3.

**[[Poison]] on an enemy** ignores block entirely and ticks every turn. Against
something that defends a lot, poison gets through when attacks do not.

**[[Vulnerable]] on an enemy** makes your attacks hit 50% harder. Apply it first,
then hit — not the other way round.
