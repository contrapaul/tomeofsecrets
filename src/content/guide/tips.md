# Tips

Things that took other players a few runs to work out.

## In a fight

- **Block before the big hit, not after.** Block vanishes at the start of your next
  turn. Holding a shield card for later wastes it; play it on the turn the enemy
  says it is going to hit you.
- **Kill the small one first.** Three enemies hitting for 4 is worse than one
  hitting for 12, because killing one removes its damage from every future turn.
- **Read the badges before you play anything.** Decide how much damage is coming,
  then decide whether to block it or race it.
- **[[Vulnerable]] and [[Weak]] are damage.** Weak on an attacker saves you health
  every turn it lasts; Vulnerable on a target makes everything after it hit harder.
- **Energy you do not spend is wasted.** It does not carry over. A cheap card played
  is worth more than a perfect card held.

## Between fights

- **Your health does not come back on its own.** Camps and some events are the only
  way to heal, so take one when you are low even if an upgrade is tempting.
- **Skip cards on purpose.** A deck of 12 good cards is stronger than 25 decent
  ones, because you draw your best cards more often. Skipping is a real choice.
- **Take the elite when you are healthy.** Elites drop a relic, and relics are what
  make a run snowball. Fighting one at half health is how runs end.
- **Spend your gold.** Gold left over at the end of a run buys nothing.

## Across runs

- **Losing earns Lore too.** Every run pays, so a bad run still moves the Tome
  forward.
- **Buy Pages you will actually use.** A Page unlocks cards for the pool, so a Page
  for a class you never pick does nothing yet.
- **Every enemy you kill writes its page**, and once a page is written you can steal
  that enemy's Secret card after a fight.
