# How a run works

You climb a map of a chapter, one room at a time, fighting with a deck of cards.
When you die — and you will, the first few times — you keep what you learned. The
next run starts stronger.

## A fight, in one paragraph

You and the enemies take turns. On your turn you get **energy**, and you spend it
playing cards from your hand. Attacks deal damage. Skills usually give you
[[block]], which soaks damage until your next turn and then disappears. When you
are done, you end your turn, the enemies do the thing they told you they were
going to do, and your turn comes round again.

## The three numbers that matter

| Number | Where | What it means |
| Energy | The orb on the left | What you can spend this turn. It refills every turn. |
| Block | The shield on your health bar | Damage it soaks before your health does. It is gone next turn. |
| Health | Your health bar | When it hits zero the run ends. It does **not** refill between fights. |

That last line is the one new players miss. Health carries from room to room, so a
fight you win at 5 health is a fight you nearly lost.

## Your cards are a deck, not a hand

- You draw 5 cards at the start of each turn.
- At the end of your turn, whatever is left is discarded.
- When the draw pile runs out, your discard pile is shuffled back in.

So every card you add is a card you will see again — and a card you have to draw
past to reach the good ones. A small, sharp deck beats a big, vague one.

## What the enemies are about to do

Above each enemy is a badge: its **intent**. A sword means it will attack, and the
number is how much. A shield means it will defend. Others mean it is about to
strengthen itself or weaken you. The badge is not a guess — it is what is going to
happen, so you can block exactly what you need to.

## Dying is part of it

When the run ends you earn **Lore**, and Lore buys Pages in the Tome: new cards,
new relics, new ways to start. Nothing is wasted. Read *Tips* next, or tap any gold
word anywhere in the game to have it explained.
